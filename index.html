IIWhere wind comes from

Every breeze beginswith the Sun.

i

Uneven heat

The Sun warms land, sea and sky unevenly, at the equator more than at the poles.

ii

Air on the move

Warm air rises and cooler air rushes in to fill the gap. That rush is wind.

iii

A spinning planet

Earth's spin curves those currents into the great global wind belts.

So wind power is really solar power, delivered by air.

VThe Betz limit

Why no turbine cancatch all the wind.

i

Stop it completely

If a turbine stopped the air dead, no new air could flow through it.

ii

Don't slow it at all

If the air passed through untouched, the turbine would catch nothing.

iii

The sweet spot

Slow the wind to one-third of its speed. Real turbines reach about 45–50%.
